The instrument kit of parts

Rather than 200 unique spacecraft, fifteen instrument modules snap together in different mixes, the way one set of Lego bricks builds many models. Costs shown are mass-production targets around unit 1,000 of a run, converted at 1 USD to A$1.55 (August 2026 indicative rate).
