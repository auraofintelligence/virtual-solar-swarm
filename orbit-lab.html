Orbit lab

Every body owns a bubble of space where its gravity is in charge, called its Hill sphere. Park inside the bubble and you stay; drift too near its edge and the Sun (or the parent planet) steals you. This lab finds the bubble for any target and lets you slide a satellite through it.
