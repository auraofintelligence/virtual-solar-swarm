The scale

No rule fixes 200 targets, or four satellites to a picket, or 1,300 in the fleet; those are where this study started counting. On this page both the fleet total and the policy for spending it are dials in your hands. The page does the arithmetic and shows you what each setting changes; what any of it is worth is yours to weigh.
