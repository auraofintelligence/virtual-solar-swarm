Launch windows

You cannot leave for another world whenever you like. The cheap path is a long curve that arrives where the target will be, so Earth and the target have to line up first, like merging into moving traffic. Each lining-up is a launch window, and this page finds them.

Cruise times here are the fuel-cheapest ferry route, which grows silly for deep targets (thousands of years to Sedna). Real missions to the far dark would fly faster, meaner paths and brake harder, or settle for one screaming flyby. The cheap-path number stays useful as the honest baseline for comparing doors.
