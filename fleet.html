The fleet

Six tiers of attention, from a hundred satellites wrapped around the Sun down to the census entries that carry none of their own. Drag the dials and watch the whole fleet re-total. The source design sketches 1,500 to 2,000 spacecraft; the defaults here build about 1,300, which is only the starting point on the scale. Slide the survey dial off zero and watch what promoting the whole census would cost.
