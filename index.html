Design the swarm that would watch the whole solar system

This studio models a single idea: put a small standing crew of satellites around each of the 200 most interesting objects in the solar system, so nothing out there ever goes unwatched again. Every page is a working model you can pull on.

The family, drawn by code from each body's real properties. Click any face on the catalogue for its dossier.

This is a design study, not a mission. Nothing on this site has been built or launched. The maths is teaching-grade mechanics: right enough to compare options and plan shapes, not to fly a spacecraft. The about page lists every approximation.

The idea in one breath

Two hundred funded targets, about the number of countries on Earth, each with a standing crew: a baseline picket of four satellites, one over each pole and one over each side, rising to dense swarms of fifty at busy worlds and a hundred wrapped right around the Sun, so a solar storm could never again leave from the side we cannot see. Around the funded core rides the census: every named moon of the giant planets and a deeper sweep of small worlds, watched by the host swarms. All up the model builds a fleet of around 1,300 spacecraft, and every one of them eventually wears out, which is why replacement gets its own pages.
